Meet Amplify Tyee

🎶 A.M.P.L.I.F.Y. = Advancing Music Programs, Learning, and Inspiration For Youth

Amplify Tyee is a nonprofit dedicated to supporting the Tyee Music Department—band, orchestra, choir, and theater.

🧥 Buy a Music Hoodie Support Our Students
✨ Our Mission
To create a vibrant, inclusive music community where students thrive creatively and musically.

🎯 Goals Include

  • Organizing volunteers, fundraising, field trips, and events
  • Offering financial support for instruments, uniforms, and equipment
  • Improving communication between families and directors

💖 Support Our Students

We now accept donations, company matching gifts, and volunteer hour matches. Every contribution goes directly toward instruments, uniforms, equipment, and opportunities for Tyee music students.

PayPal or Zelle

Donors can send money anytime using our email address amplifytyee@gmail.com or by searching “Amplify Tyee.” Please include a note in the memo field (e.g., “Donation from [Name]”). Donors will receive a receipt via email after the transaction.

Donate with PayPal Donate with Zelle

✉️ Checks

Donations by check should be made payable to “Amplify Tyee” and can be mailed to the school or handed to any board member for collection.

🏢 Company matching gifts

Many employers match charitable donations dollar for dollar. Check with your employer to double your impact.

⏳ Volunteer hour matches

If you’ve chaperoned a trip—log your hours and double your impact!
