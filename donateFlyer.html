Amplify Tyee

Tyee Middle School Music Booster Club

Advancing Music Programs, Learning, and Inspiration For Youth

Amplify Tyee is a parent-founded 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to supporting the Tyee Music Department—band, orchestra, choir, and theater.

Our Mission

To create a vibrant, inclusive music community where students explore, grow, and excel.

Goals Include

  • Support student experiences through fundraising, performances, field trips, and volunteer coordination
  • Offer financial support for instruments, costume attire, sheet music, supplies, guest lectures, and more
  • Strengthen communication and partnerships between families and the music department

Ways To Support Our Students

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, we welcome tax-deductible donations, company matching gifts, and volunteer hour matches. Every contribution goes directly toward instruments, equipment, guest lectures, performances, and other enriching opportunities for Tyee music students.
