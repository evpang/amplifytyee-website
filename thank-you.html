Thank You!

Thank you for your hoodie order and for supporting Amplify Tyee. Every purchase goes straight back to the Tyee Music Department, and families like yours are the reason our band, orchestra, choir, and theater students have what they need to perform.

Pre-ordered hoodies are delivered to students in class, about a month after the order window closes.

💖 Every Note Matters

Haven’t donated yet? There’s still time

Hoodie sales cover a slice of what the music program needs. Our annual Every Note Matters campaign covers the rest. Our projected expense for this year is $20,000, which includes a grant of $5,000 to each music director, totaling $15,000. Directors have used their grants to replace worn out instruments, purchase choir stoles, theatre play scripts, etc. The remaining $5,000 covers event transportation subsidies, teacher training, guest lecturers, fundraising expenses, and adminstration fees. We have raised about $1,000 so far, so we need your help to reach our goal. Suggested donation is $100 per student, but any amount is welcomed.

Donations are tax-deductible, and many employers match them — which doubles your impact.

Donate with PayPal Donate with Zelle

Zelle and PayPal both reach us at amplifytyee@gmail.com. Checks can be made payable to “Amplify Tyee” and sent to the school or handed to a board member. Questions? Just email us.
